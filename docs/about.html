About

I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Centre for the Study of African Economies in the Department of Economics at the University of Oxford. I completed my PhD in Public Policy at the School of Public Policy, Oregon State University, in 2026. My work sits at the intersection of the economics of education, child labor, climate change, and program evaluation.
